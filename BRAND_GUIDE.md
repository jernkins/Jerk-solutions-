# Jerk Solutions — Brand Guide

## Brand essence
A futuristic, premium and practical technology brand with a global ambition and a focus on useful digital products.

## Slogans
- Primary: **Innovate. Build. Empower.**
- Supporting tagline: **Technology beyond limits.**
- Campaign option: **Smarter Tech. A Better Tomorrow.**

## Logo concept
A JS monogram with an orbital ring communicates connection, progress and global reach. Keep it clear at small sizes. Avoid stretching or busy backgrounds. The supplied SVG is a starting concept, not trademark clearance.

## Colours
- Obsidian Black: `#050B16`
- Deep Navy: `#071D3B`
- Electric Blue: `#0066FF`
- Neon Cyan: `#00D1FF`
- Metallic Silver: `#C0C7D2`
- White: `#F5F8FF`

## Typography
Use a clean sans-serif such as Inter, Montserrat, system UI or Arial. Bold headings and keep body text readable.

## Voice
Confident, clear, curious, helpful and honest. Explain products plainly and avoid exaggerated claims.

## Values
Innovation · Excellence · Integrity · Accessibility · Impact

## Social bio
Building useful digital tools, software and learning experiences.  
Innovate. Build. Empower.  
Technology beyond limits.
